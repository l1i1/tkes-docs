# Impact Program

The Tokeness impact program supports projects that use AI for open-source, public-interest, education, research, and social value.

## Suitable Projects

- Open-source tools and public infrastructure.
- Public-interest or social-impact services.
- Education and research experiments.
- Non-commercial community projects.

## What to Prepare

When applying or contacting Tokeness, prepare a short description of the project, expected usage, public links, and the intended AI workload.

## Contact

Email <contact@tokeness.io> with project context and expected usage.
