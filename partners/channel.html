Channel Partners ​

Tokeness supports a standard API + Key distribution model, suitable for partners who already have developers, enterprise customers, or aggregation platform resources.

Cooperation Model ​

ItemDescription
Integration methodStandard API + Key model
System compatibilityCompatible with New API aggregation systems
Account capabilitiesDistribution account features are consistent with individual platform users
Settlement methodPrepaid; customer call consumption is deducted from the account balance in real time
Support1-on-1 business contact; technical issues are handled with engineer assistance

Admission Policy ​

Based on local business materials, the current channel policy includes:

ItemCurrent terms
DepositNo deposit required
Minimum top-upNo minimum top-up amount
BalanceTop-up balance remains valid long-term
SupportFirst-batch quality distributors may discuss first-order top-up support

Tiered Discounts ​

Channel partners can receive tiered discounts based on monthly volume. The example structure from the current price table:

Monthly volumePartner discount
10,000 < volume < 50,0005% off
50,000 ≤ volume < 100,00010% off
volume ≥ 100,00015% off

Official-relay channels do not participate in partner discounts; final terms are subject to formal business confirmation.

Onboarding Materials ​

Channel partners typically need:

  1. Channel price table.
  2. Company promotional materials.
  3. Cooperation agreement or contract template.
  4. API integration guide.
  5. Customer service and technical support contact.

This repository already includes channel promotional materials and contract templates, which can be distributed according to the actual business process.

Contact ​

Email: contact@tokeness.ai

Business communication can be further arranged through the channels in existing business materials.
